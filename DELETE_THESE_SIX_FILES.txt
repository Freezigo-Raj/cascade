== WHAT CHANGED IN SESSION 150 ==

THE 7PM BUG. A 7pm meeting read 1:30pm, which is 5h30m out, which is your own
offset. Every instant in this engine is LOCAL WALL CLOCK with the offset written
on the end; the Android calendar provider hands over a TRUE epoch instant. One
was passed straight into the other, so 13:30 UTC got labelled 13:30+05:30.

An all-day event is deliberately NOT shifted: the provider stores it as UTC
midnight of the date, so its date already reads correctly, and shifting would
move a phone west of UTC to the previous day.

A second fault of the same kind, found while fixing the first: the
missing-from-calendar window test compared a wall clock against a true instant,
so it was out by the same 5h30m at both edges of the window.

YOUR EXISTING CALENDAR TASKS FIX THEMSELVES. A task still following Google has
its date taken from the calendar on every import, so the next Import now
rewrites the wrong time. One you had already edited is detached and keeps YOUR

WHAT A SHORT REPEAT IS NOT. It is not an alarm nagging you.

  auto-snooze          one occurrence, rung again, stops after auto_max
  every 10 minutes     a schedule; each one closes and the next is a new row

ONE LIMIT, AND IT IS NARROWER THAN I FIRST SAID.

A repeat only ever has ONE open occurrence, so only one ring is armed at a
time. The next occurrence is created when the current one is closed, and it is
armed the moment that write lands. So what matters is whether the press reaches
the web half.

PRESSING DONE ON THE ALARM ARMS THE NEXT ONE. The receiver calls `surface()`,
which brings the app forward on purpose, precisely so the press is applied
where it can be seen. If the app was already running it heard the press live
and applied it immediately instead.

  Pressed at the lock screen   Ring stops   App comes forward   Next one armed
  Done                         yes          yes                 straight away
  Push                         yes          yes                 straight away
  Snooze                       yes          no                  same occurrence,
                                                                re-armed in Kotlin
  Cancel                       yes          NO                  at next app open
  Dismiss                      yes          NO                  at next app open
  nothing — ignored            auto-snooze  no                  at next app open
                               to auto_max,
                               then
                               UNANSWERED

Cancel and Dismiss do not bring the app forward, by a decision in session 128:
somebody cancelling a thing at a lock screen has said what they wanted to say,
and pulling the app to the front would take the screen off whatever was on it.

SO THE LIMIT IS: an IGNORED alarm, or one you Cancel or Dismiss from the lock
screen, leaves the next occurrence unarmed until you next open the app. At
daily intervals that is invisible. At five-minute intervals the chain pauses
while the app is closed. Unchanged design, newly visible; changing it means a
re-arm chain in Kotlin and a new policy, so it is not here.
